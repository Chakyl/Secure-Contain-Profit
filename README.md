# Secure Contain Profit 
Secure Contain Profit  is an incremental economy pack inspired by the worlds and mechanics of **SCP** and **Lobotomy Corporation**. Expand your facility to ship Enkephalin for the Moonlit Company, harvested from dangerous abnormalities.

[![bap_madefor_badge](https://i.ibb.co/C5gCk6Rw/bap1-madefor-64h.png)](https://discord.gg/r3uTFGfkgf)

## Features
**Abnormalities**: 3 tiers of evolving abnormalities with increasing difficulty: Verdant, Amber and Maroon.
**Base Building**: Expand out your facility to contain and suppress breaching abnormalities.
**Quests**: Detailed quests will guide you through the progression of the modpack.
**Combat**: Fill out your arsenal of weaponry to take down any threats and raids in your facility...
